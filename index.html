Good To Know

Deposit & Cancellation Policy

A few friendly ground rules that keep the schedule fair for everyone.

Deposit

  • A $20 deposit is required to confirm any appointment.
  • The deposit is applied directly to the cost of your service.
  • Deposits are paid securely online at the time of booking.

Cancellations

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • Cancellations with 24+ hours' notice: deposit is fully refunded or moved to your next visit.
  • Cancellations under 24 hours: deposit is forfeited.

Late arrivals

  • A 15-minute grace period is included for every appointment.
  • Arriving after 15 minutes may mean a shortened service or reschedule, at the tech's discretion.
  • Arriving after 20 minutes is treated as a no-show.

No-shows

  • No-shows forfeit their $20 deposit in full.
  • A new deposit is required to book any future appointment.
  • Two or more no-shows may require prepayment for future services.
